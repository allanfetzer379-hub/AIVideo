FAQ

Common questions

QDo you work directly with our clients?
No. We work behind the scenes. Your agency owns the client relationship. All communication and deliverables go through your team.
QCan you help with scripts?
Yes. If you already have a script, we can produce from it. If you only have a brief, product link, or campaign idea, we can help turn that into a usable video script. For deeper creative strategy, we recommend using a Campaign Pack.
QCan you do AI face swap?
Yes, when the likeness, source material, and intended use are approved by the client or talent involved. We do not support unauthorized face swaps or misleading impersonation.
QWhat AI models do you use?
Our workflow may include Seedance 2.0, Kling 3.0, AI avatar tools, face swap tools, editing tools, and other AI production systems depending on the project. We choose the tool based on the creative goal, quality requirement, and budget.
QWhat kinds of videos are best for AI production?
AI works especially well for:
  • Product videos & explainer videos
  • AI spokesperson content
  • Paid social variations & campaign hooks
  • Local business promo videos
  • SaaS feature explainers
  • Educational content
QDo you only make short videos?
No. Most agency demand starts with short-form content because Reels, TikTok, Shorts, and paid social ads require frequent new creative. But we can also support longer explainer videos, website videos, product walkthroughs, and campaign videos.
QHow fast can you deliver?
Most projects can be delivered within a few business days depending on volume, complexity, and revision needs. For recurring agency clients, we can set up a regular production schedule.
